# Gates of Olympus 1000

Everything here was measured from the original (48 fps recordings, the PHJOY / MegaPerya / Pragmatic Replay captures, and replays `DPB5uJIXrW` (regular art) and `QFSrr6V6s9` / `QjIGOjoO1e` (Xmas build)). Values marked measured or fitted are not guesses: change them only by re-measuring.

## Layout of this folder

- `GatesOfOlympus.vue`: the stage template; its script only creates the engine and wires the composables together.
- `composables/`: `useSpinFlow` (spin → tumbles → multiplier collection → scatter/big win → free spins round, `onSpin`), `useTumbles` (tumble loop, pay lines, win list), `useReels` (grid, drop plans, landings, Zeus, strikes, retime/slam), `useScatterAnticipation`, `useMultiplierCollect` (orb and pillar flights, tally merge), `useWinCounter` (tumble/controls win count-ups, big win), `useFreeSpins` (round state, max-win cap, windows, transition), `useAutoplay`, `useBuyFeature`, `useDemoCredit`, `useGameMenus` (one open menu at a time, `blocksSpin`), `useGameAudio`, `useGameLoading`, `useTopMessages`, `useSpeedToast`. Later composables take earlier ones as arguments; callbacks into ones created later (`onSpin`) are passed as closures.
- `engine/`: pure logic and tuned constants. `symbols.ts` (paytable, symbol/orb tiers, art boxes and rig placement), `grid.ts` (grid generation, tumble refills, free-spin outcome scripting), `timing.ts` (speeds, drop/tumble timing, holds, `sleep`/`until`), `collect.ts` (multiplier flight keyframes), `freeSpins.ts`, `bigWin.ts`, `winFx.ts` (stage geometry, fx timelines, rig configs), `topMessages.ts`, `assets.ts` (asset base, preload list, loader progress).
- `components/`: one SFC per visual piece. `spine/`: the Spine 3.7 runtime (`spineLite.ts`), WebGL renderer (`spineGl.ts`, `useSpineGl` for context loss and disposal) and rig loaders (`rigs.ts`). `text/`: baked glyph tables and label recipes.
- `createGridEngine(random)` (`engine/grid.ts`) owns everything random: the symbol key counter (`newKey`), the per-spin orb-reel set and every draw, including the base spin's trigger and late scatter (`baseSpin`, `dropLateScatter`). The game passes `Math.random`; tests pass `seededRandom(seed)` (`engine/random.ts`) and fail if the engine calls `Math.random`. Keep the order of `random()` calls when refactoring: the 20k-spin summary in `engine/__tests__/grid.spec.ts` (hit rate, payback, triggers, orb spins) is an inline snapshot of the fitted model, so a changed number means the odds changed. Update it (`npx vitest -u`) only for an intended re-fit.

## Stage and layout

- UIs use the original's pixel grid: stage 1920 × 1080 design px (1cqw = 19.2 px), panels define `--p` = one screenshot px. Sizes are stage-relative only (`cqw`, or `min(100vw, 100vh * 16 / 9)` outside a container), never mixed with `px`, so browser zoom keeps proportions. Thin rings are inset `box-shadow` (`--ring`), not `border`: Chrome rounds borders under 1 CSS px up to 1 px, which at 500% zoom paints 5 device px.
- Center the stage with margins, not `translate(-50%)` (half-pixel blur). The flash layer is a non-isolated twin box for `mix-blend-mode: screen`.
- Reel cells are 217.82 × 164.19 design px: the 5 rows are tighter than the board window (y 104–936), so `.reels` (top 10.141%, height 76.014%) is centred in it and `clip-path: inset(-0.673% 0)` clips at the window edge, not the cell grid (top-row rings and hourglasses poke above their cell). Keep in sync with `WinEffects`/`OrbWinEffects` positions, `REELS` in `winFx.ts` and `FX_BOARD_CLIP.y` (0.2221 cells = the old frame clip).
- `reel/*.webp` are the original's static idle sprites (`S01`–`S11`, trimmed, drop shadow baked in); `REEL_ART` boxes and the grid were fitted by template-matching all 30 cells of an original screenshot (sub-px residuals), so swapping the art means refitting the boxes.
- The board and the win-list box are the original sprites. Board (`Playground/RA/Reels`): `board-shadow` (the `shadow` sprite 9-sliced to 1264 × 822 with borders 54/51 larger than half the 78 × 77 tile, so NGUI maps the middle span backwards; rebuilt with that mapping) and `board-back` (`reels`, 1226 × 781), both centred at UI (−0.5, 19) under the reels, and `board-front`, the 26-piece `MovieController` gold frame (1777 × 999 canvas centred at (3.5, 0), cropped to x 229, y 49), drawn over the symbols and under the win effects. Win list (`WinList`): `history-back` (`bg` 273 × 299; halo and rim removed, triangles recoloured to (79, 41, 34) at 0.665, solved from screenshots) under the rows and `history-frame` (`frame` 248 × 259) over them, both centred at (−756, −211.1). All are 1 atlas px per UI unit.
- `background.webp` is the `gool_basegame` rig rendered alone at 4/3 scale at its full extent (stage px x −661…2581, y −401…1130). It is registered to the stage (`.scenery` has the stage's size), so wider windows reveal more of it. Never `object-fit: cover` it: off 16:9 that zooms and shifts the art, and the side panels' torn edges then show the city instead of the pillar. Windows taller than 16:9 show black below the stage.
- Win list rows (4 shown, `--row` 2.72cqw) were sampled from screenshots: a 5-line double-gold band on top, no side borders, one gold-cream text gradient. A row never wraps: `WinList` measures it at full size (again after `document.fonts.ready`) and sets `--fit` to shrink its font (counts, icon and gaps are in `em`).

## Reel timing

- Timings come from 48 fps recordings. Spin drops (`SPEEDS`): normal staggers columns left→right and rows bottom-first on the way out and in; quick/turbo move all columns together, and rows 2–4 of each column fall as one stack with rows 0–1 ~70 ms behind (`inRow` `[87, 70, 0, 0, 0]` is measured, not a typo). The new grid starts falling before the old one has drained, so the old grid stays rendered as a `leaving` layer. Every landing dips 12% and settles (`land`).
- Speed changes apply mid-spin (the speed button stays enabled). Each spin drop keeps a per-cell `dropPlan` (delay, duration, ease); `retimeDrop` gives every cell that hasn't started moving the new speed's profile, starting now (cells in flight finish untouched), and moves the bolt landings with it. CSS animation timings follow those per-cell vars live. During a win highlight, switching to turbo moves the `WinFx` timeline `start` to turbo's `TURBO_BURST_LEAD` and drops the pop; switching to turbo also finishes running win counts. Waits use `until()`, which re-reads its deadline, so retimed phases end on time.
- Slam stop (normal speed only): Space, or a left click on the stage outside buttons and the controls band (`SLAM_IGNORE`), while the spin drop is running retimes the drop to `SLAM_DROP` so everything lands now with its `land` dip. It does nothing during win highlights, explosions or refills, and the spin button never slams (`GameControls` emits `stop` only from Space). The ignore check walks `composedPath()`, not `target.closest`: starting a spin swaps the button's icon before the click bubbles up, so the target is already detached.
- Tumble refill (`dropColumn`, `TUMBLE_FRAMES`, from PHJOY kymographs): every symbol eases in over 1 frame then falls 1.686 frames per row, and dips with `land` on arrival; no symbol waits for its column to settle. Columns start 1 frame apart left→right; inside a column the survivors start bottom-up 2 frames apart, the first new symbol 0.8 frame after that slot, then new ones 2.4 frames apart. Each cell gets its own `--fall-delay`/`--fall-ms`/`--fall-ease` (a `linear()` built per distance), and strikes in a tumble land at that cell's `landsIn`. Cells that don't move get no `drop` class, so they don't dip.
- Turbo cycle (`300spins_noantebet.mp4`, 285 spins): a losing spin starts the next one 605 ms after its own start, which our drop + `land` already takes, so turbo autoplay skips `AUTOPLAY_GAP_MS` (250, kept for wins and the other speeds) after a spin that won nothing; with a scatter on the board the original holds 940 ms, so a losing turbo spin with a scatter waits `TURBO_SCATTER_HOLD` (335 ms) after landing (manual Space too).
- Every speed holds `STRIKE_HOLD` (350 ms; the turbo recording measured ~560 but it felt long) after the last multiplier strike lands before checking wins or ending the spin, and a dealt multiplier delays the drop by `dealLead` (520 normal, 680 quick/turbo).

## Win effects

- Win border and explosion are sprite sheets (`fx/border.webp`, `explode.webp`) lifted from the recordings by subtracting the empty board, played additively (`lighter` + `plus-lighter`) on a canvas one half-cell larger than the reels, clipped to the board frame. One frame = one 47.97 fps video frame (`FX_FRAME_MS`), sized by `FX_SOURCE_CELL` (191.5 × 145.4 video px per cell).
- The border sheet comes from `Pragmatic Replay … 19-03-01.mp4` (the 21-cell win at 15.8 s): every side of every cell plays the same line animation, rotated clockwise and stretched along its length. Each sheet pixel within 20 px of the line (fading by 30) is one real side (a median averaged away the glitter), farther out the median of all clean sides; corners blend over 24 px, the inner glow fades 16–24 px inside the cell, board-edge sides are sampled only 12 px outside the board. It is played frame for frame: in the original every cell runs the same fixed sequence in sync (5-frame fade-in, a dim gap stepping clockwise every 3 frames on a 26-frame cycle, 6-frame fade-out, lines ~10 px past the corners), so don't animate it procedurally. Every winning cell draws its full border, so a shared edge gets both cells' lines, brighter and wider like the original.
- Timeline in `winFx.ts`; `win-pop` (grows to 1.23, snaps back) and `win-vanish` keyframes are measured, not eased. Quick drops like turbo but plays every effect at normal speed (`fxRate` 1); turbo (0) skips them except the explosion, which plays at normal speed `TURBO_BURST_LEAD` (140 ms; the original is 80, felt too abrupt, 200 felt slow) after the win with no border or `win-pop` (WinFx `start` jumps to just before `burstAt`, `border: false`), so a tumble takes the same 583 ms from burst to refill.
- Every winning symbol plays its original Spine win animation (`SYMBOL_RIGS`: gems `gool_lv_1_orange` … `gool_lv_5_blue`, crown `gool_hv_1`, hourglass `gool_hv_2`, ring `gool_hv_3`, chalice `gool_hv_4`; ~1.5 s) from the highlight start (`symbolWins`, cleared only on refill or a switch to turbo, never when the animation ends: dropping the key let `win-pop` swell the symbol just before it exploded); turbo skips them. Skeletons are the base64 `spineJSON` of `gool_*_SkeletonData`, packed into `fx/sym-*.{json,webp}` (non-white setup slot tints baked into tinted region copies); placement fitted against `reel/*.webp` (0.330 sprite px per Spine unit).
- `CellSpine` draws a rig inside a cell but teleports its additive canvas to `.cell-light` on the stage, placed over the cell each frame and clipped to the board window: inside `.reels` (clip-path, `isolation`) plus-lighter has no board to add to, so dark additive pixels painted as dark specks.
- Board win amounts (`WinAmount`) are the original `MarioFloatingText`: the `HelveticaMario` bitmap font (`ui/win-digits.webp` + `winFont.ts`) at 84 UI units, one UI unit = 1080 / 999 design px. Each label starts 60 units below the winning cell and plays `MarioShowLineWin` (1.25 s Hermite curves: rise 0 → 40 → 120, scale 0.5 → 1.1 → 0.95 → 0.8) with the `WidgetColorAnimator` alpha (in by 0.03 s, out from 0.625 s), sampled by `hermite()`. The font has no ₱: ours is drawn into the atlas at (100, 272) in the font's style (rounded P, open bowl outlined in blue, two bars sticking out both sides with one blue slit; chosen over the alternatives tried), so board amounts use `formatPeso`.
- Tumble-bar and controls WIN count-ups are linear, start with the win highlight and last `BAR_COUNT_MS` (600) whatever the amount. Max win is 15,000× bet (`MAX_WIN_X`): tumbles stop once it is reached, every multiplier is still collected, then the total is capped, the bar shows `MAX WIN` and jumps straight to the cap (the controls WIN (`controlsWin`) counts at normal speed in normal and quick, instantly in turbo except for the multiplied or max-win total), and a red `MAX WIN` row tops the win list.

## Multipliers

- Multiplier strike (`StrikeEffects`, `STRIKE_FX`) replays the original `gool_summon_effect_1`–`_4` Spine rig from its data: `fx/strike.webp` repacks the additive JPEG atlas (black = nothing): 16 bolt frames, 4 per variant (green, blue, purple, red), keyed at 0.0333 / 0.1333 / 0.2 / 0.3 s, gone at 0.3667, plus `effect_sparks1` (purple, bone at local y 374.1, ×2.4) and `effects_sparks2` (red, at −10.04, 159.56, ×2). Sorting the atlas names with `Object.entries` puts `10`–`15` first, which once scrambled the variants. Variant = threshold index (`[0, 10, 50, 100]`): green 2–8, blue 10–25, purple 50, red 100+.
- Strike placement: root at the cell's centre x and 323.7 design px above its centre, 1.0795 px per local unit, bolt frame at ×2 (589 × 1066 px), above the board, additive over everything (`z-index` 5), unclipped. It starts `STRIKE_FX.lead` (200 ms) before the orb lands, so the bolt hits the empty slot and the orb falls into the impact. The mirrored twin (1 of 5 strikes, no pattern) is picked by `strikeFlip` hashing the symbol key, stable across `retimeDrop`.
- Red strikes play the `shake` clip on the board, frame, reels, rig and tumble plate (not side panels, logo, Zeus or controls): keys 0.1–0.5 s scaled into `STRIKE_SHAKE` design px, mirrored with the bolt. One shake at a time: each red strike restarts it, and orbs dealt with the spin drop don't shake. `StrikeEffects` writes `--shake-x`/`--shake-y` on the stage. Verified against the replay's own PIXI renderer: 1.4–3.3 / 255 mean difference, 0 px offset.
- Orbs: `reel/m*.webp` are the static `S12_0`–`S12_4` sprites (`info/m*.webp` for the paytable). Orb cells stack by tier (`tierVars`), so a higher multiplier covers a lower one. Each tier's `art` box and the diamond position were fitted to a screenshot.
- The label (`MultiplierLabel`) is the original 4-layer `LabelMultipleLayers` recipe in the game's label font (Go Noto Kurrent-Medium, baked into `multGlyphs.ts`). Thicknesses are literal (a ×2 version looked neon). Gradient spans y −72.5…6.1 (measured); the black shadow is drawn first, widened to 4.2 units around the pink drop only, blurred 0.9 at 0.85 (diamond `labelShadow` 0.6); `x` at 0.9 of its size (`GLYPH_SCALE`); squash 0.864 / 0.96 = 0.9. em = 0.2494 × orb width (0.2506 on the diamond); `--mult-em` is a % of the cell width, so height must come from `aspect-ratio`. Only the shadow is filtered: filtering the whole label resampled it at fractional offsets and blurred it. Arial Black is too heavy.
- Orb win animation is the original Spine 3.7 data (`gool_wild_multiplier_win_*`, `gaoo_1000_multiplier_win`) repacked into `fx/mult-*.{json,webp}` and played by `spineLite.ts`, which mirrors spine-ts 3.7 (weighted meshes, `noRotationOrReflection` bones, translate-only transform constraints in update-cache order, the 10-segment bezier table and float32 key times, so a frame exactly on a key shows the previous attachment). `OrbWinEffects` draws normal slots on one canvas and additive ones on a `plus-lighter` canvas where later normal slots erase with `destination-out` (= Spine's blending). Spine units → sprite px: 0.2505 (wings) / 0.5 (diamond), origin at `ORB_RIGS.origin` (rms 10).
- Collection (`engine/collect.ts`): the number rides `bone5` / `multiplier_number` until `SWELL.leave` (375 ms), swells linearly to 1.5× (`SWELL.to`), leaves the orb at `SWELL.flyAt` (615 ms) and flies a fixed `COLLECT.fly` (530 ms) wherever it is: travel on `ZOOM_EASE` (`0.26, 0.02, 0.62, 0.58`), size near the peak then shrinking (`k²`) to the bar digits' size. The next orb starts `ORB_RIGS.nextAt` (0.78 s wings; 0.62 diamond, unmeasured) into the previous one. It lands on the multiplier's slot in the bar (a hidden `ghost` copy of the final `x N` layout, `COLLECT.landOffset` right of centre); the bar switches layout `slotLead` before the first landing; the flyer shrinks and fades into the slot (`ABSORB_FRAMES`) while the new number swells to 1.45 at 37% of 190 ms. The merge starts 530 ms after the last landing (`absorb` 200 + `hold` 330); both parts slide together over `COLLECT.slide` (210 ms), the `x` hides 100 ms in, they rest 70 ms, then the product (or `MAX WIN`) bounces to 1.47 at 37% of 240 ms. All of it runs at normal speed on every speed. All measured on the 19-03-01 recording.
- Every orb that comes to rest after moving plays its `landing_*` / `gaoo_1000_multiplier_land` animation (`MultTier.land`) from touchdown (`markLandings` stores that time per key, `retimeDrop` updates it), drawn by `OrbLanding` inside the cell so `land` and tier stacking apply; the static sprite hides once the rig draws. During the landing the label goes into `CellSpine`'s `over` slot, teleported after the additive canvas (inside the cell the glow painted over it), riding the body bone (`ORB_RIGS.bodyBone`), not the number bone (the text only lifts ~4 px with the orb, while `bone5` swings ±12 px). The wings `origin` x (0.5344) was nudged so the rig at rest matches the sprite within 0.5 px.

## Audio, credit, loading

- `PlayView` lazy-loads the game, showing `GameLoader` at 0% until the game reports progress; `loadStages` fakes a 2.5 s progress curve that waits for `loadGameAssets` at 100%.
- `useLoopingMusic` uses Web Audio for gapless loops; `blocked` until a `click` (not `pointerdown`). It mutes through the gain while `document.hidden` (suspending the context would flip `blocked` and show the mute icon).
- The game uses its own ₱100,000 demo credit (`DEMO_CREDIT` in `useDemoCredit`; each spin deducts the stake and pays its win; free spins pay when the result window opens), topped up by ₱100,000 when below `REFILL_BELOW` (₱10,000) while idle (not playing, buy popup closed). The top-up counts up over `REFILL_MS` (`useCountUp`, ready only while `refilling`). It is not the Sakura wallet on purpose (the user's choice, 2026-11-02): client-written spin results can't be verified by rules, and settling an interrupted spin needs the outcome decided server-side (Cloud Functions on Blaze). The controls BET shows the stake (`stake`: bet × 1.25 with the ante), and big win tiers use it.

## Free spins

Built from the user's rules (2026-10-31), the MegaPerya recordings (`… 11-35-44` full round, `14-12-54` turbo, `14-15-19` quick big wins, `14-15-50` / `14-16-54` retriggers) and replay `DPB5uJIXrW`. Rules and placements are in `engine/freeSpins.ts`, generation in `engine/grid.ts`.

- **Scatters** are placed by script, never drawn per cell: at most one per reel, none in refills except the late 4th. A base spin triggers with `triggerChance(ante)` (no ante ≈ 1 in 494; ante ×2.25 ≈ 1 in 220) and lands 4 / 5 / 6 scatters (90 / 9 / 1%); otherwise 1 / 2 / 3 teasers on 30 / 15 / 5% of spins, the same with or without the ante. A bought round's trigger spin lands exactly 4 on reels 2–5 of a `quietGrid` (no orbs, no symbol 8+ times), so it pays only the scatters (3× / 5× / 100× bet for 4 / 5 / 6).
- **Late scatter**: `withLateScatter` withholds the 4th scatter in `lateScatterShare` (50%) of 4-scatter triggers when the drop has a win in a reel without a scatter, and `dropLateScatter` puts it in that reel's first refill, so trigger odds don't change.
- **Anticipation** (no suspense hold; tumbles keep their pace at every speed, as the user confirmed for the original): from the explosion of a base-game tumble with 3 scatters, they play `gool_scatter_anticipation` once at `anticipationRate` 1, held at `anticipationPeak` (0.4667 s, eyes fully lit; `CellSpine` `hold` freezes bones and colours while attachments keep running on `frameT`), with `CellSpine` `swell` scaling the rig to `anticipationSwell` (1.05) with a quadratic ease-out (linear or smaller values were tried and rejected). `stopAnticipation` fires `anticipationReleaseMs` (250) before the refill drops and plays out over those 250 ms; the refill drops `anticipationDropMs` (1.2 s, the user's choice) after the explosion. Never looped. Held scatters that fall in a refill skip the landing replay.
- **Odds** (2026-11-01, fitted to 481 ante spins from three autoplay recordings, combined 26.4% won, 35.4% base payback; 313 no-ante turbo spins left as is): base orbs in `ORB_CHANCE` (0.12%) of dropped cells (~1 in 25 spins); a reel that dropped an orb gets no other from the spin drop, and from a refill only once every reel has one (`orbReels`, reset by `randomGrid`); values from `ORB_WEIGHTS` (mean 5.4). Each base refill cell copies a pay symbol still on the board with `REFILL_STICKY` (25%) so tumbles chain like reel strips. Simulated (1 M spins, ante): 27.0% win, base ≈ 35% of the stake; with free spins ≈ 71%, a buy ≈ 97% (the rules screen states 96.5%). Changing any of these (or `PAYS`) needs a re-simulation. Not done yet: a per-reel stack chance for the 2–3 stacked symbols seen in recordings.
- **Round outcome is picked first** (`pickRoundTarget`, × bet): 95% 7–200×, 4% 200–1,000×, 0.9% 1,000–5,000×, 0.1% 5,000–14,999× (mean ≈ 94×), max win 1e-7, skewed low per band. Each free spin is the best of 60 simulated candidates (`freeSpinCandidate`: a pay cluster, 0–3 orbs solved after the tumbles are simulated, optional retrigger scatters); ~40% aim at zero. Scripted spins replay their simulated refills through `freshQueue`, so tumbles match the simulation. Totals land near the target.
- **Free spin grids** (from 7 bought rounds: totals 241, 149, 102, 38, 25, 23, 9×; orbs mostly 2–5×; round multipliers 2–31×): columns drawn top-down repeating the cell above with `FS_STACK` (35%); orb values from `FS_ORB_WEIGHTS` in `FS_DRAWN_VALUES` (75%) of candidates, otherwise solved toward the target (`orbValues`); a spin aiming at zero gets an orb in `FS_DEAD_ORB` (45%), `FS_NEAR_MISS` (60%) of the time as a near miss (`nearMiss`: 7 of one symbol including a 2–3 stack, the orb right under it). Refills are not stacked; free spins place their own orbs, at most one per reel, and refills never deal orbs.
- **Max win** is 15,000× the base bet for the whole round (`winCap` = cap − round won so far; 12,000× the stake with the ante). Reaching it ends the round: `endFreeSpins` drops FREE SPINS LEFT to 0 once the cap is certain, every multiplier is still collected, the bar shows `MAX WIN` with that spin's capped win, the red row the full max win, a retrigger is ignored, and the result window follows.
